I'm S. Rahul Prasath, an Electronics and Communication Engineering student at M.A.M. College of Engineering and Technology, Siruganur, Tiruchirappalli. My development journey began with learning Python programming and quickly expanded into full-stack web development with Flask, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Alongside software, I continue to work on electronics projects involving Arduino, sensors, and circuit design.

I'm particularly interested in combining Electronics + Software + Web Technologies to build innovative solutions that bridge the physical and digital worlds.
